No account · works offline · stays on your iPhone

One day, four tides

Built for the moments symptoms actually happen: in the middle of a meeting, at 3 AM, and in a short appointment.

Morning

Tap the sun when a hot flash comes

One tap logs it, with the time. Your pinned symptoms sit right below, one tap each. Change how strong it was, or undo, straight away.

  • Time a hot flash as it happens
  • Notes, and what came before it
  • Your own sea, drawn from your own logs
Afternoon

See your periods and stronger days

A calendar with your periods and a small wave under each day that shows how strong it was. Tap a day to see what you logged.

Night

Quick, then back to sleep

After 10 PM the screen goes dark and dim, with night sweats and waking up first. Something at 2 AM counts as last night, so your days make sense.

The visit

A clear report for your doctor

Your top symptoms, how often and how strong, your periods, treatments and sleep, as a PDF or on screen, with your questions for the visit.

  • Patterns over weeks and months
  • Before and after a new treatment
  • Only ever compared with your own history

Private, and plainly so
