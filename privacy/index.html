Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Wavelle is a menopause and perimenopause tracker. It has no account and no server of its own. This page says exactly what the app does with information.

Your logs stay on your iPhone

Everything you log (symptoms, periods, treatments, sleep, mood, notes, your own trackers, questions for your doctor and the name on a report) is stored only on your iPhone. We never receive it.

  • iCloud sync (off by default). If you turn it on, your logs are also kept in your own private iCloud database by Apple, so they appear on your other devices. We can't see them.
  • Apple Health (off by default). If you turn it on, Wavelle saves symptoms Apple has a type for, with how strong they were, and period flow, and reads sleep and period flow, only for the items you allow on the iOS screen. This stays between Wavelle and Apple Health on your iPhone.
  • Reports and exports are made on your iPhone and go only where you send them.

What is sent, and what isn't

Wavelle sends two kinds of anonymous information:

  • Crash reports (Sentry). If the app crashes or freezes, a report of the code that failed and the device model and iOS version is sent to Sentry. It has no health data, no screenshots, no text you typed, and IP addresses are removed.
  • Anonymous usage counts (TelemetryDeck). Counts like "a log was saved", "a report was made" or "a guide was opened", with fixed values only (for example which part of the app). They never include symptoms, dates, notes, names, anything you type, or any identifier that could be linked to you. There is no switch for these counts.

Wavelle has no ads, no tracking, no advertising identifier and no data brokers. We never sell or share data.

Permissions

Notifications, Apple Health and Face ID are asked only when you open those features, and Wavelle works fully without them.

Deleting your data

Settings › Your data › Delete all data removes everything from your iPhone (and from your iCloud when sync is on). Deleting the app also removes the data on the phone.

Children

Wavelle is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
