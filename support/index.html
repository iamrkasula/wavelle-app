Common questions

When does a day start?

At 6 AM. Something you log at 2 AM counts as last night, on the evening before.

How do I log without opening the app?

Add the Wavelle widget (touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget) and tap the sun, use the Control Center button, or say "Log a hot flash in Wavelle" to Siri.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Turn on iCloud sync in Settings, or make a backup in Settings › Your data and open it on the new iPhone with "I've used Wavelle before".

How do I share my logs with my doctor?

Open Report, choose the months, and share the PDF, or use Show on screen during the visit.

Reminders or Apple Health don't work

They may be turned off in iOS. Wavelle shows the exact steps on the Reminders and Apple Health screens in Settings.

Is my data private?

Yes. No account, and your logs stay on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

Reporting a problem

Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, the Wavelle version (Settings › About) and what you did just before it happened. Please don't send health details you'd rather keep private; we don't need them.
